Why Visit Sri Lanka?

Sri Lanka is known as the Pearl of the Indian Ocean, offering a mix of culture, wildlife, beaches and mountains. From ancient temples to tropical coastlines, it is one of the most diverse travel destinations in Asia.

Best Time to Visit Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka is a year-round destination. The south and west coasts are best from November to April, while the east coast is ideal from May to September.
